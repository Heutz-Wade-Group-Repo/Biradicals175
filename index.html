Spectrum

First-derivative, field-swept style display on a frequency axis, powder-averaged over θ with a Gaussian line of σ = 2 MHz (4 MHz peak-to-peak in the derivative). The g offset is fixed at Δ = 20 MHz so the weak and strong regimes have a fixed boundary.

Pendulums & Normal Modes

Each pendulum represents a spin; the 20 MHz detuning makes A (the higher-frequency spin) slightly shorter than B. The spring models the flip-flop term B = J − d/2. When |B| ≫ Δ, the normal modes are pure in-phase (T₀) and anti-phase (S) motion.

Distance

Pendulum spacing follows the point-dipole relation r = (52.04 / ν_dd)1/3 nm. Here J acts as an independent knob; in real molecules it decays exponentially with distance and depends on the bridging pathway.
