Spectrum

First-derivative, field-swept style display on a frequency axis, powder-averaged over θ with a 2 MHz Gaussian line. The g offset is fixed at Δ = 20 MHz so the weak and strong regimes have a fixed boundary.

Pendulums

Each pendulum is a spin; the 20 MHz detuning makes them slightly different lengths. The spring is the flip-flop term J − d/2, where d = νdd(1 − 3cos²θ). Energy moved from A to B peaks at B²/(Δ² + B²) and repeats every 1/√(Δ² + B²).

Distance

Pendulum spacing follows r = (52.04 / νdd)1/3 nm. J is treated as an independent knob here; in real molecules it falls off steeply with distance and depends on the bridge.
